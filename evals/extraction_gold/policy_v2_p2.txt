4 Travel
4.1 Flights
Book economy class for flights under 6 hours. Business class needs approval from a director.
4.2 Per diem
The per diem covers meals and local travel for each full day away from the home city.
City  Per diem (₹ a day)
Mumbai  2,000
Delhi  1,800
Bengaluru  1,800
Other cities  1,200
4.3 Hotels
Stay in a hotel from the approved list. A hotel outside the list needs approval from the travel desk.
