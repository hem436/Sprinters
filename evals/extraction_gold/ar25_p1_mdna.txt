Management Discussion and Analysis
Revenue
Revenue from operations grew 12.0% to ₹11,256 crore in FY2024-25, from ₹10,050 crore in FY2023-24. The FY2023-24 figure is restated from ₹10,000 crore after a change in how partner fees are recorded.
Growth came from higher payment volumes, where segment revenue rose 12.9% to ₹7,000 crore, and from lending, where revenue rose 10.5% to ₹4,256 crore as the loan book grew in tier-2 cities.
Margins
Employee costs rose 11.1% as we hired engineers for the new lending platform. Finance costs rose with higher borrowing for the loan book. Profit for the year grew 12.9% to ₹5,250 crore.
Risks
The main risks are credit losses in the lending book, a change in payment interchange rules, and fraud on new payment products. The risk committee reviews each of them every quarter.
Outlook
For FY2025-26 we expect revenue growth of 10% to 12%, led by payments. We will keep finance costs below 2% of revenue.
